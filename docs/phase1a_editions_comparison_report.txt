======================================================================
Edition F  = Riyad as-Saliheen, ed. Dr. Mahir Yasin al-Fahl (Dar Ibn Kathir, Damascus/Beirut, 1428 AH / 2007) - file riyad_fahl
Edition R  = Riyad as-Saliheen, ed. Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (Mu'assasat al-Risalah, 3rd ed., 1419 AH / 1998) - file riyad_risalah

1. HEADLINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Hadith entries in F: 1896  (numbered 1..1896 with no gaps; 372 chapters)
Hadith entries in R: 1893 blocks  = 1892 real hadith + 1 cross-reference stub (see 2e)
Matched one-to-one by TEXT (not by printed number): 1892
In F but not in R: 4      In R but not in F: 1 (stub only)
=> The two files are NOT identical in count: F has 1896 hadith, R has 1892 real hadith (4 fewer).
   Of the 1892 shared hadith, the printed numbers agree except for a +1 shift in F#456-854 and a handful of typos in R (section 3).

2. THE FIVE STRUCTURAL DIFFERENCES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
a) F#855 (chapter 132) - 'ان اولى الناس بالله من بدأهم بالسلام' (Abu Umama). In F this hadith is printed twice, as #855 (ch.132) and #858 (ch.133).
   R prints it once (as #858). So R lacks the first copy of a repeated hadith.
b) F#1374 (chapter 240) - Jabir 'ishtara minhu ba'iran fawazana lahu fa-arjaha' (muttafaq 'alayh): the text is absent from R altogether.
c) F#1375 (chapter 240) - Abu Safwan Suwayd ibn Qays 'jalabtu ana wa-Makhrama al-'Abdi bazzan min Hajar...': absent from R altogether.
   R's printed numbers also skip 1374 and 1375 (1373 is followed by 1376), so the numbering says they exist but the file has no text.
d) F#1749 (chapter 335) - Abu Hurayra 'idha da'a al-rajul imra'atahu ila firashihi fa-abat...' - a repeat of F#281 (chapter 35).
   R prints it once (as #281); the repeat in chapter 335 is absent.
e) R#456 (chapter 54) is not a hadith: it is the closing cross-reference 'hadith al-'Irbad ... wa-qad sabaqa fi bab al-nahy 'an al-bida'.
   F contains exactly the same words at the end of #455 ('wa-fi al-bab ahadith kathira minha: hadith al-'Irbad ...'), unnumbered.
   R numbers it separately, which pushes every later R number up by 1 (F#456..854 = R#457..855) until the missing F#855 absorbs the shift.

f) Placement: F#1673 (Abu Mas'ud al-Badri, 'naha 'an thamani al-kalb wa-mahri al-baghiy wa-hulwani al-kahin') is the last hadith of chapter 303 in F.
   R prints the same hadith with the same number (1673) but its text sits before the heading of chapter 303 (inside chapter 302), ahead of #1668-1672. Same hadith, different position in the file.

Chapters whose hadith counts differ between F and R: ch.54 (F 10 / R 11), ch.132 (F 7 / R 6), ch.240 (F 9 / R 7), ch.302 (F 11 / R 12), ch.303 (F 6 / R 5), ch.335 (F 1 / R 0)

4. WORDING OF THE 1892 SHARED HADITH
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Method: both texts stripped of diacritics, punctuation, footnote markers (F), page markers (F) and Qur'an references; compared word by word;
each difference classified. Spelling conventions (e.g. الرحمان/الرحمن, ومئة/ومائة) and attached connector letters are reported separately from real wording differences.

Hadith by overall status (one status per hadith):
    1125  IDENTICAL                        identical word for word (diacritics ignored)
      90  SPACING_ONLY                     identical words, only word spacing differs (e.g. ما لا / مالا)
     128  HAMZA_ALIF_SPELLING_ONLY         identical except hamza/alif/ya spelling (e.g. أ/ا, ى/ي, ة/ه)
      50  ORTHOGRAPHIC_CONVENTIONS_ONLY    only spelling conventions differ (e.g. الرحمان/الرحمن)
     107  CONNECTOR_LETTERS_ONLY           only attached connector letters (و/ف/ب/ل) and spelling conventions differ
      25  BLESSING_FORMULAE_ONLY           only blessing formulae differ (e.g. تعالى / عز وجل / صلى الله عليه وسلم)
     367  WORDING_DIFFERENCES              at least one real wording difference (different word, extra word or minor word form)
    1892  TOTAL

=> 1215 hadith are word-for-word identical; 310 differ only in spelling/connector/blessing; 367 contain at least one real wording difference.

Differences by type (individual differences, all hadith):
      97  WORDING (different word(s))
     138  EXTRA WORD(S) only in Fahl
     123  EXTRA WORD(S) only in Risalah
     160  MINOR WORD FORM
      44  BLESSING FORMULA
     152  CONNECTOR LETTER (و/ف/ب/ل...)
     141  SPELLING CONVENTION
   words present only in F: 261;  words present only in R: 298
   hadith with at least one SUBSTANTIVE difference (different / extra words): 269; of these, inside the quoted prophetic text of the body: 94

Notes on interpretation:
  * 'Extra words' are mostly one-word insertions (the Arabic particle إنّ/إني, هذه, تعالى, a repeated 'قال'): different manuscripts/riwayat followed by each editor.
  * Differences in the TAKHRIJ TAIL (after 'رواه ...') are usually editorial (e.g. 'رواه الترمذي وقال: حديث حسن صحيح' vs a shorter form), not the hadith text.
  * Every difference is listed with its surrounding words in phase1a_wording_differences.csv (filter 'importance' = SUBSTANTIVE).

6. VERDICT FOR PHASE 1a
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The two editions contain the same book (1892 hadith in common, in the same order, in the same chapters apart from one boundary case).
They are not literally identical: F has 4 entries that R lacks (2 are repeats of hadith printed twice in F, 2 are absent altogether),
and about 20% of the shared hadith contain at least one real word-level difference, most of them one-word differences between riwayat.
Under the rule you set on 2026-11-22 (section 7), only 11 of the 1892 shared hadith (0.6%) count as having a real difference.
F (1896, clean numbering 1..1896) is the best master numbering for the next phases.

7. YOUR WORDING-DIFFERENCE RULE APPLIED TO THE 1892 SHARED HADITH (added 2026-11-22)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule as you gave it: 'at least one real wording difference is not always a real difference, even though we can have the numbers for it;
at least 3 real wording differences will be considered a real difference, and furthermore even at least four real wording differences will be
considered a real difference if 90% of the wording matches.'
Reading used (the only one that keeps both clauses meaningful): a hadith pair has a REAL DIFFERENCE when it has >= 3 real wording differences and the
two texts match less than 90%, or >= 4 real wording differences when they match 90% or more. The grid below lets you apply any other reading.

Definitions
  real wording difference = a different word, an extra word, or a minor word form (الخير / خيرا). NOT counted: spelling conventions (الرحمان / الرحمن),
     hamza/alif/ya spelling, word spacing, attached connector letters (و ف ب ل), blessing formulae (تعالى / عز وجل / صلى الله عليه وسلم).
  scope 'body' (headline) = the hadith text, i.e. chain + matn; the takhrij tail after 'رواه ...' is editorial and excluded. 'quoted' = only inside the quotation marks
     (the Prophet's own words). 'all' = body + takhrij tail.
  match % = 100 x (1 - words inside real differences / words of the longer edition's text), per hadith.
