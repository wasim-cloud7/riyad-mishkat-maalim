Riyad as-Saliheen: HASAN-level hadith (hasan / hasan li-ghayrihi / jayyid / hasan sahih) - 51 of 1896
=====================================================================================================
Benchmark: a hadith counts as SAHIH when Albani and/or Arna'ut grade it sahih (either one is enough). Evidence ladder: A1/N1 = inferred from Bukhari/Muslim attribution, A2 = Albani's own label in his Riyad file, A3 = Albani's label on the matching Mishkat entry. Arna'ut's gradings are NOT in the supplied Risalah file, so his side currently rests on rung N1 only.
Statuses: IN_CONFIRMED = same hadith found; IN_PROBABLE = probably the same hadith in another narration/abridgement; UNCERTAIN = weaker resemblance; DIFF_COMPANION = same wording but a different Companion (distinct hadith); NOT_IN = nothing comparable found.

[F#43 | Risalah no. 43] Ch.3 باب الصبر
   Albani: HASAN [A3] «حسن» | Arna'ut: UNGRADED [N0]
   Sources: J,T | P2
   Mishkat: IN_CONFIRMED (#1566) | Ma'alim: IN_CONFIRMED (block 2861, book no. 3320)
   وعن أنسٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِذَا أَرَادَ الله بعبدِهِ الخَيرَ عَجَّلَ لَهُ العُقُوبَةَ في الدُّنْيا، وَإِذَا أَرَادَ اللهُ بِعَبدِهِ الشَّرَّ أمْسَكَ عَنْهُ بذَنْبِهِ حَتَّى يُوَافِيَ بِهِ يومَ القِيَامَةِ». وَقالَ النَّبيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إنَّ عِظَمَ الجَزَاءِ مَعَ عِظَمِ البَلَاءِ، وَإنَّ اللهَ تَعَالَى إِذَا أَحَبَّ قَوْمًا ابْتَلَاهُمْ، فَمَنْ رَضِيَ فَلَهُ الرِّضَا، وَمَنْ سَخِطَ فَلَهُ السُّخْطُ». رواه الترمذي، وَقالَ: «حديث حسن».
